# Responsibilities

The responsibilities for a junior analyst include:

- Monitor and investigate alerts
- Configure and manage security tools
- Develop and implement basic IDS signatures
- Participate in SOC working groups
- Create tickets and escalate where necessary

# SOC

- Preparation and Prevention: it is important to stay up to date with news and cyber events. Prevention methods include gathering threat intelligence on threat actors and latest TTPs, it also includes maintaining firewall signatures, patching vulnerabilities and managing block lists.
- Monitoring and Investigation: SIEM and EDR are proactively used for monitoring network activities.
- Response: Take coordinated action like isolating hosts and terminating malicious processes.
